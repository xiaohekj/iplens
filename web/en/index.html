    How IPSpot Works & Frequently Asked Questions

    8 QUESTIONS

    IPSpot cross-checks RIR registries (ARIN, RIPE, APNIC) and RDAP country records against 12 geolocation feeds to verify native vs broadcast routing, combining ASN type, cloud keywords, rDNS, and Shodan traits to separate residential ISPs from datacenters.

    Powered by Cloudflare Radar 7-day ASN bot traffic statistics combined with network baselines, broadcast checks, proxy/VPN/Tor feeds, AbuseIPDB, and StopForumSpam reports for transparent 0–100% scoring without penalizing clean residential ISPs.

    Every signal (Proxy, VPN, Tor, Datacenter, Mobile, Abuse) is cross-checked across proxycheck.io, official Tor Onionoo relays, StopForumSpam, IP2Location, ipquery.io, ip-api.com, and Shodan ports, displaying the exact verified or matched sources.

    GeoIP providers (MaxMind, IP2Location, IPInfo, ipapi.is, GeoJS, etc.) update at different intervals and handle anycast or broadcast routing differently. IPSpot displays up to 12 independent feeds side by side and uses consensus matching.

    Locally collects FingerprintJS v4, Canvas, WebGL, Audio, font hashes, screen DPR, and WebDriver/Headless signals, then compares your browser timezone, language, and WebRTC output against your exit IP.

    DNS leak detection probes randomized 32-character subdomains to bypass caches and capture your true recursive resolvers, while WebRTC testing queries multiple global STUN servers to check for exposed local or public IPs.

    Using the target IP coordinates, IPSpot queries OpenStreetMap Nominatim and Photon reverse geocoding in real time to return nearby street, city, state, country, postal code, and timezone data with one-click copy.

    ASN Live streams real-time BGP route announcements and withdrawals from RIPE RIS Live; AI Diagnosis provides risk advice based on verified metrics; the Open JSON API supports zero-auth IP, domain, and ASN queries.
